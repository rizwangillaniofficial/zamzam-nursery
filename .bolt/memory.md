# Project memory

## Product intent & audience

- Plant nursery eCommerce landing page; high-end, responsive, production-worthy.
- Target audience: premium/premium-conscious plant buyers seeking a luxury botanical shopping experience.
- Key features: product filtering, cart drawer, interactive plant quiz, coupon system.

## Brand personality & voice

- Rebranded from "Verdora" to **ZamZam Nursery**.
- Brand voice: premium, refined, botanical luxury.
- Logo: gold crescent cradling an ivory-and-sage leaf inside a forest-green rounded tile; wordmark "ZAMZAM" with subtitle "Premium Plant Nursery."
- Coupon code: `ZAMZAM10` (10% off); order confirmations and social handles use zamzamnursery branding.
- Cart storage key tied to new brand name; old cart data resets after rebrand.

## Visual design & UX direction

- Luxury botanical aesthetic with gold accents on forest-green/ivory/sage palette.
- Components: header with logo + nav, footer, mobile menu, cart drawer, product grid with filtering, plant quiz.
- Uses botanical illustrations as imagery fallback when stock photography unavailable.
- Icons via lucide-react; logo also used as favicon.

## Decisions, constraints & rationale

- Rebrand applied across all brand surfaces: header, footer, mobile menu, favicon, SEO metadata, about-story, Instagram handle, newsletter, FAQ, coupon, order confirmation.
- Professional online photos were attempted but stock photo service returned errors; polished botanical illustrations retained as current imagery.
- Real photo swap deferred to future turn when photo service recovers.

## Open questions & assumptions

- Photo source/availability unresolved — user wants professional online images; currently blocked by service errors.
- No explicit user-provided logo asset; logo is a custom-designed mark.
